About

Business, technology and operations — working as one system

Alexander is a senior project and business transformation leader with more than 20 years of experience across multinational companies and high-growth businesses in Automotive, Banking, Insurance and Telecommunications. He has built teams and operations from scratch, launched digital platforms, and led nationwide network expansion programmes for international brands.

His work sits at the intersection of business, technology and people. Rather than managing projects in isolation, Alexander designs the operating systems around them — defining requirements, coordinating software engineers and vendors, implementing CRM/ERP platforms, and putting KPI and governance structures in place so that results are repeatable, not accidental.

He works fluently with cross-functional teams — sales, marketing, operations and technology — and is equally comfortable setting strategy with executive stakeholders and writing the business requirements that development teams build against.

Methodology

How I work

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    Understand the current state — processes, systems, stakeholders and constraints — before proposing anything.

  2. 02

    Design

    Define the target operating model, requirements and success metrics in terms the business and IT both understand.

  3. 03

    Plan

    Build a realistic roadmap with milestones, ownership, budget and risk controls in place from day one.

  4. 04

    Execute

    Coordinate teams, vendors and engineers to deliver against the plan, with governance to keep things on track.

  5. 05

    Measure

    Put KPI and reporting structures in place so results are visible, not anecdotal.

  6. 06

    Improve

    Use what the data shows to refine the process, the system and the plan — continuously, not once.

Selected Case Studies

Transformation in practice

Automotive · Network Development

Building a nationwide service network

Challenge. Launch a new automotive service brand nationwide with no existing sales, no advertising budget, and strong competition from global players.

Approach. Defined the rollout model end-to-end — partner onboarding, training, CRM and lead-generation requirements — and coordinated regional managers, marketing and operations against a shared roadmap and KPI framework.

Result. Grew the network to roughly 300 service centres and ~RUB 100M in new sales within three years, helping move Russia from 28th to 2nd place globally for the brand.

Startup · Commercial Operations

Building commercial operations for a digital startup

Challenge. Turn an early-stage automotive-services idea into a working, scalable digital business with no existing sales organisation.

Approach. Built the sales function from scratch, worked directly with software engineers to define CRM and automation requirements, and introduced Agile-style collaboration and KPI reporting through Jira.

Result. Established a functioning commercial operation with structured reporting and quality metrics, supporting the company's strategic planning and investor presentations.

Operations · Digital Transformation

Operations and digital transformation

Challenge. Build large-scale operational infrastructure capable of reliable service delivery across a cross-functional organisation.

Approach. Implemented CRM/ERP systems, redesigned core processes, and put budgeting, vendor management and KPI controls in place across the operation.
